FAQ

Optical shop in Jaipur —
your questions answered

Everything you might ask before visiting our optical stores for spectacles, eyeglasses, sunglasses or contact lenses.

Which is the best optical shop in Jaipur?
Khanna Vision has been a trusted optical shop and opticians in Jaipur since 1961, with two stores — on M I Road and at Choura Rasta, Bapu Bazar. We stock spectacles, eyeglasses, sunglasses and contact lenses from leading brands, with eye testing and expert fitting by three generations of opticians.
What are your store timings? Are you open now?
Both our optical stores are open Monday to Saturday, 10:30 AM – 9:00 PM, and Sunday until 8:00 PM. Walk in any day — no appointment is needed for an eye test.
Which eyewear and spectacle brands do you stock?
We carry Ray-Ban, Oakley, Vogue, Versace, Emporio Armani and Carrera, plus Essilor and Zeiss lenses — including prescription glasses, sunglasses, rimless frames and clip-ons for men and women.
How long does it take to get new glasses?
Most prescription glasses are ready in as little as 2 hours at Khanna Vision, so you rarely have to wait or come back another day.
Do you sell contact lenses and provide eye tests?
Yes. We stock contact lenses (Acuvue, Biofinity and more) and offer professional eye testing, contact-lens fitting, and prescriptions for single-vision, bifocal and progressive lenses.
Where are your optical stores located in Jaipur?
Store 1: Shop No. 334, M I Road, Jaipur. Store 2: Shop No. 278, Choura Rasta, Bapu Bazar, Biseswarji, Jaipur. Both are easy to reach — get directions from the maps above.
